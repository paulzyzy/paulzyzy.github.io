About

Researcher in Learning-Enabled Autonomous Systems

I am a member of the Miao Group at the University of Connecticut, where I work on learning systems for intelligent agents. My recent projects study how large language models can support multi-agent reinforcement learning, how dynamic routing can improve mixture-of-LoRA experts, and how vision-language-action models can become more robust in embodied settings.

I received an M.S. in Data Science from the University of Pennsylvania and a B.Eng. in Ocean Engineering and Technology from Zhejiang University.
