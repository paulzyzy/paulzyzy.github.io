Yuan Zhuang 庄元

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Connecticut. My primary advisor is Prof. Fei Miao, and my co-advisors are Prof. Dongjin Song and Prof. Jonathan Shihao Ji.

My research focuses on efficient foundation models and adaptive computation, including long-context language modeling and mixture-of-experts. I also work on reinforcement learning and embodied AI.

In summer 2026, I interned at Microsoft Research, mentored by Dr. Xiaodong Liu, developing adaptive-depth methods for test-time-training language models. Previously, I worked on vision-language-action models at JD.com. I received my M.S. in Data Science from the University of Pennsylvania and my B.Eng. in Ocean Engineering and Technology from Zhejiang University.

I expect to graduate in December 2026 and am seeking full-time Research Scientist, Research Engineer, and Applied Scientist opportunities.
